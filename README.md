An entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Printer Apology"](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-the-printer-apology-60229153): one line, at most 120 characters — an apology note from an office printer to the people who keep jamming it, feeding it cheap paper and swearing at it.

## The entry

> Sorry I jammed — you feed me bargain paper, swear at me, and still I'm the only coworker who never calls in sick.

113 characters. The printer apologises for the jam, names its real grievances (bargain paper, being sworn at), and lands the punch at the end: for all the abuse, it is the one coworker who never calls in sick.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it to stdout.
